OULAD Experiment Summary (Phases 1–5)

Overview

Dataset: OULAD (Open University Learning Analytics Dataset)

Experiment type: Natural educational disparities (no artificial bias
injection)

Pipeline: 1. Baseline training 2. Influence diagnostics 3. Direction
comparison 4. Repair selection 5. Final untouched-test audit

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Overall Conclusions

-   The complete five-phase pipeline successfully transferred from the
    synthetic experiment to a real educational dataset.
-   No artificial bias was injected and no oracle model was available.
-   Influence functions estimated an oracle-free fairness repair
    direction.
-   The fairness gradient on OULAD was much smaller than in the
    synthetic benchmark.
-   Consequently, both the raw-gradient and Hessian-preconditioned
    repairs produced only modest improvements.
-   Equalized Odds improved slightly while predictive performance
    remained essentially unchanged.
-   The repairs modified fewer than 0.4% of test predictions,
    demonstrating that the intervention remained highly localized.

This experiment complements the synthetic benchmark by evaluating the
proposed machine-unlearning framework under naturally occurring
educational disparities.
