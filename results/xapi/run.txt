OVERALL CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------

The xAPI-Edu-Data experiment demonstrated that the proposed
influence-guided machine unlearning framework successfully
computed meaningful fairness repair directions while fully
preserving predictive performance.

Compared with the baseline model:

    Smooth EO:
        0.003012
            ↓
        0.002920

    Accuracy:
        unchanged (0.864583)

    Balanced Accuracy:
        unchanged (0.798352)

    Macro-F1:
        unchanged (0.816875)

    Prediction Changes:
        0

The repair therefore produced small probability-level fairness
improvements without changing any final classification decisions,
illustrating the conservative behaviour of the framework on a
high-performing real educational dataset.
